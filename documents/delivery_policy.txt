Delivery usually takes 30 to 45 minutes depending on distance and traffic.
Delivery partners are assigned once the restaurant confirms the order.
Live tracking is available in the app once a delivery partner is assigned.
Delivery charges are 20 rupees for orders under 300 rupees and free above that.
Deliveries beyond 8 kilometers from the restaurant are not currently supported.
During heavy rain or festival days, delivery times may extend by 15 to 20 minutes.
Customers can leave delivery instructions such as gate codes or landmark notes at checkout.
Contactless delivery can be selected, in which case the order is left at the door.
