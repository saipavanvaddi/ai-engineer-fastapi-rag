Manakarto restaurants are open from 10 AM to 11 PM daily, including weekends.
Customer support is available via chat or phone from 9 AM to midnight.
For urgent order issues, use the in-app chat support for the fastest response.
Email support is also available and typically responds within 24 hours.
On public holidays, some restaurant locations may have reduced hours;
this is shown on the restaurant's page before ordering.
Escalations that are not resolved by chat support within 30 minutes
can be escalated to a supervisor by requesting a callback in the app.
