Refund eligibility depends on the cancellation stage and payment method.
Refunds for prepaid orders are processed within 5 to 7 business days.
Cash on delivery orders are not eligible for refunds if cancelled after acceptance,
since no payment was collected in advance.
Refunds can be issued as wallet credit, which is instant, or as a bank refund, which takes longer.
Wallet credit refunds can be used toward any future order with no expiry.
If an order arrives damaged, missing items, or incorrect, a partial or full refund can be requested
within 24 hours of delivery through the Help section.
Refund status can be tracked from the Orders tab under Refund Status.
