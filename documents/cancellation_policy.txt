Customers can cancel an order before the restaurant accepts it.
Once the restaurant accepts the order, cancellation may not be possible.
If food preparation has already started, cancellation is normally not allowed.
Orders can be cancelled from the Orders tab by tapping Cancel Order.
If a restaurant is closed or unable to fulfil an order, it is auto-cancelled and fully refunded.
Repeated cancellations after acceptance may result in a temporary restriction on placing new orders.
For a cancellation request after acceptance, customers should contact support directly,
and approval depends on how far preparation has progressed.
